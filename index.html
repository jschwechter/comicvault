📸 How to scan (no comic knowledge needed):
1. Tap Front — photograph the whole front cover, flat and centered
2. Tap Back — always photograph the back cover, even for slabs (through the plastic is fine) — there's a small code back there on every comic that tells us the exact issue and variant
3. If it's in a hard plastic case (a "slab"), also tap Label — photograph just the white/colored strip at the top with the grade number
4. Wait for the green checkmark, then tap Save — you don't need to check or fix anything else
That's it. Anything the app isn't sure about gets flagged automatically for review later — you never need to guess.
